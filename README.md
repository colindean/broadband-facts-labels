# broadband-facts-labels

A (~~probably~~ incomplete) attempt to standardize broadband facts labels.

The FCC under President Trump [proposed on November 3, 2025 ending the broadband label program][fcc-25-74a1].

I may revisit this attempt if both of these:

1. the program survives the Trump Administration with the
   "display labels in customer account portals" and
   "make labels available in machine readable format" requirements intact.
2. One of these:
   1. Google does _not_ eliminate XSLT support from Chrome,
      as [proposed and scheduled for implementation by the end of 2026](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/web-platform/deprecating-xslt),
   
   1. [Mason Freed's XSLT polyfill](https://github.com/mfreed7/xslt_polyfill)
      ([xslt-polyfill on npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/xslt-polyfill))
      proves to be a suitable replacement with long-term support.

[fcc-25-74a1]: https://web.archive.org/web/20251222213616/https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-25-74A1.pdf
